Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

DeskMove ("we", "the app") is published by Diviner Labs. This policy explains what the app collects and why. We collect the minimum needed to run the app.

What we collect

  • Your setup answers: daily desk hours, available time, focus area, level and areas to avoid. Used to recommend routines.
  • Workout progress: which routines you start and finish, skipped exercises and the reason you choose, ratings, and your streak. Used to show progress and improve the content.
  • An anonymous account ID created automatically, so your progress can be backed up. We do not ask for your name. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address that provider shares.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription or purchase. Payments are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat.
  • App diagnostics and usage events (such as screens opened and crashes), used to fix bugs and understand which features help.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect health records, location, contacts or photos.
  • DeskMove is general wellness content, not a medical service.

Service providers

We use Supabase (data storage), RevenueCat (subscription status), Google Firebase (diagnostics and analytics) and Apple / Google (app distribution and payments). They process data only to provide their service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Most data stays on your device. Backed-up data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all backed-up data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account and data. You can also email us to request deletion. Cancelling a subscription is done in your App Store or Google Play account.

Children

DeskMove is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change. Questions: see Support.
